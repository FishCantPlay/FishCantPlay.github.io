GROUP PROJECT

A relationship management game about a group of students trying to make a game together without completely destroying their friendships in the process.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

THE GAME

Group Project is a relationship management game centred around a group of students attempting to create a game together. The player takes control of Nott, a crow who acts as the group's programmer and general mediator.

Throughout the project, the player makes choices in conversations and situations involving the rest of the team. These decisions affect the relationships between Nott and the other characters, with different choices causing some characters to become happier while potentially upsetting others.

The game focuses on the messy social side of group work rather than traditional combat or progression. Characters have different personalities, opinions and ways of approaching the project, meaning that keeping everyone happy is not always possible.

The visual direction combines cute animal characters with a slightly messy student-project aesthetic. The writing takes inspiration from character-driven games such as Night in the Woods, while the animal characters use distinct speech patterns and personalities to make each member of the group feel different.
