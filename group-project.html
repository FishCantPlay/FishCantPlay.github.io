01 / THE BRIEF

What's the project?

A game made together

This was a university group project focused on creating a relationship-management game. The project involved developing a shared game concept around a group of characters and the way their relationships could change through player choices.

The aim of this page is to show the project as a whole, but also make it clear which parts I personally worked on.
