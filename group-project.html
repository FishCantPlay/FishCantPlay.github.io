GROUP PROJECT

A relationship management game about a group of students trying to make a game together, while their personalities, opinions and relationships constantly get in the way.

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

THE GAME

Group Project is a relationship management game centred around a group of students attempting to create a game together. The player takes control of Nott, a crow who acts as the group's programmer and mediator.

Throughout the project, the player makes choices during conversations and situations involving the other members of the team. These choices affect relationships, with some characters becoming happier while others may become upset.

The game focuses on the social side of group work, with each character having their own personality, opinions and approach to making games. Keeping everyone happy is not always possible.

The visual style combines cute animal characters with a slightly messy student-project aesthetic. The writing focuses heavily on character personality and relationships.
