GROUP PROJECT / STUDENT GAME DEVELOPMENT

GROUP PROJECT

A relationship-management game about making a game with people who all have their own ideas.

A character-driven project about teamwork, communication and the chaos of trying to make something together. The player's choices affect the relationships between the members of the team and can ultimately change where the project ends up.

01

THE PROJECT

THE TEAM

A group of students trying to make a game together while bringing very different personalities and ideas to the table.

RELATIONSHIPS

Choices affect how characters feel about each other. Keeping everyone happy is much harder than it sounds.

THE GAME

A relationship-management experience where dialogue and decisions shape the team's relationships and eventual endings.

CHOICES

Different responses can make one character happy while upsetting another.

ENDINGS

Relationship values can lead toward different outcomes, including the possibility of the project falling apart.
