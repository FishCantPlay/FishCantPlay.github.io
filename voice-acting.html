Another creative interest

Voice & Performance

Giving characters a voice.

Alongside my interest in character art and modelling, I also enjoy the performance side of character creation. I have a background in theatre and would like to explore voice acting for games in the future.

01 / Background

The performance side

I've spent several years involved in theatre, including around six years of theatre-school experience and performing in major roles.

That experience is one of the reasons I'm interested in voice acting. I enjoy the way a character's personality can come through in their expressions, dialogue, delivery and the way they interact with other people.

I'm still at the beginning of developing this area and haven't made a voice acting showreel yet.

Current position

Still developing

My main professional focus remains 3D character modelling, with 2D art as a secondary specialism.

Voice acting is something I'd like to develop alongside those skills.

02 / Future development

What I'd like to explore

Character Voices

Experimenting with different personalities, tones and ways of making fictional characters sound distinct.

Dialogue Performance

Exploring emotional delivery, character interactions and how dialogue can communicate personality.

Game Performance

Learning more about voice acting for games, including character dialogue and the relationship between performance and animation.

04 / Future goals

Where I'd like to go

I'd like to learn how voice performance can support the characters I design and create. Being able to think about both how a character looks and how they sound is something I find interesting.

For now, this is an additional creative interest that I want to explore as I continue developing my main skills in 3D character modelling.
