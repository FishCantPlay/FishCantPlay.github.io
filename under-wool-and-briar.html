A story of belonging, rejection, protection and the things that can become prisons when they are built from kindness.

A third-person narrative action-adventure set within an enchanted forest. Exploration, environmental storytelling, light combat and progression come together around a world filled with creatures that have nowhere else to go.

CHAPTER I

THE STORY

Somewhere beyond the edge of the world lies a forest where the unwanted are allowed to disappear.

Those who are different are sent there. Those who have nowhere else to belong eventually find their way beneath its branches.

The forest was once a sanctuary. Over time, however, the protection offered by the Wolf Mother became something far more complicated.

The player explores this world, meeting creatures that have been rejected or misunderstood while gradually uncovering what the forest has become.
