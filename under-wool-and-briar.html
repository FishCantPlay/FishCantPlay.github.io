CHAPTER I

Once Upon a Time

❧ ✦ ❧

A world beneath the branches

Under Wool and Briar is a fairytale-inspired project built around a woodland atmosphere. The title suggests a world of nature, wandering, hidden paths and things that might not be quite as friendly as they first appear.

I want the visual identity to feel a little whimsical, but with something darker beneath the surface. This is an aesthetic I enjoy exploring in my personal artwork.

This page will document the actual story, characters, world and creative decisions as the project develops.
