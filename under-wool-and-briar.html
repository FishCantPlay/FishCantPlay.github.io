CHAPTER I

Once Upon a Time

❧ ✦ ❧

A world beneath the branches

I am building Under Wool and Briar around a fairytale-inspired woodland. I want the world to feel full of nature, wandering paths and hidden things that might not be quite as friendly as they first appear.

I want the visual identity to feel whimsical, with something darker beneath the surface. This is an aesthetic I enjoy exploring in my personal artwork.

I use this page to document the story, characters, world and creative decisions as I develop the project.
