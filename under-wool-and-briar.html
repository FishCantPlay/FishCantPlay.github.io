Chapter I / The idea

Once upon a time...

Under Wool and Briar is a project built around a woodland fairytale atmosphere. The title suggests a world of nature, wandering, hidden paths and things that might not be quite as friendly as they first appear.

This page will document the actual story, characters, world and creative decisions as the project develops.

I want the visual identity to feel a little whimsical but with something darker beneath the surface, which is an aesthetic I enjoy exploring in my personal artwork.
