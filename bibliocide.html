THE GAME

A STORY THAT DOESN'T WANT TO END

Bibliocide is a first-person puzzle game centred around the idea of escapism and the comfort of stories. The player finds themselves trapped within a distorted fairytale world, moving through familiar stories that have become increasingly unsettling. What initially appears comforting gradually becomes something much harder to leave behind.
