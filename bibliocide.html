BIBLIOCIDE

A first-person puzzle game about escapism, twisted fairytales and choosing whether to face reality.

A world built from familiar stories that have become something much darker. Bibliocide explores the comfort of escaping into fiction and what happens when that comfort becomes impossible to leave.

01

THE IDEA

Bibliocide places the player inside a distorted fairytale world where familiar stories have been twisted into something unsettling.

The game follows an escape through these spaces, solving puzzles and uncovering the uncomfortable relationship between fantasy and reality.

At its centre is the idea of escapism. Sometimes somewhere fictional can feel safer than reality, but staying there forever comes with its own cost.
