Bibliocide

Every story has an ending. What happens when someone refuses to let it happen?

A first-person puzzle game about escaping twisted fairytales, confronting escapism and exploring what happens when fiction becomes something you cannot leave behind.

CHAPTER I

The Story

❧ ✦ ❧

Once upon a time...

Bibliocide is a first-person puzzle game centred around escapism and the comfort of stories. The player finds themselves trapped within a distorted fairytale world, moving through familiar stories that have become increasingly unsettling.

What initially appears comforting gradually becomes something less familiar. The project explores the relationship between stories, escapism and the point at which a fictional world stops being a safe place.
