THEY / THEM

HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, specialising primarily in character art, concept development and visual storytelling.

Characters are probably the biggest part of what keeps me interested in creative work. I like making characters that feel expressive and memorable, whether they are genuinely cute, slightly unsettling, or somewhere in between.

I also enjoy building the worlds and stories around those characters. Games give me a place to combine art, writing, design, animation and all the strange little ideas that would otherwise have nowhere to go.

CHARACTER-FOCUSED CUTE UNSETTLING STORY-DRIVEN

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is character art, but I enjoy working across different parts of game development and understanding how they connect.

CHARACTER ART

Character concepts, silhouettes, colour exploration, visual development and taking an idea from an early sketch into a finished design.

3D ART

3D modelling and asset creation, primarily using Maya, while currently developing my skills in Blender and ZBrush.

NARRATIVE

Character writing, dialogue, scripts and narrative-focused design. I especially enjoy characters with distinct personalities and relationships.

GAME DESIGN

Developing gameplay concepts, mechanics and player experiences, particularly when gameplay and narrative can support each other.

ANIMATION

Experience with 2D and 3D animation fundamentals, with a particular interest in developing stronger character animation.

AUDIO & VOICE

Music, sound experimentation and an interest in exploring voice acting and performance for games.

Programming isn't my favourite part of development, but I understand the fundamentals and enjoy knowing enough about the technical side to understand how my work will actually be used. I even coded this website myself, so apparently I can tolerate programming when there is a pretty website at the end of it.

MY CREATIVE BACKGROUND.

PRIMARY SCHOOL

WHERE IT STARTED.

Art clubs, drawing constantly and experimenting with anything I could get my hands on. Making things has always been something that came naturally to me.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

ART + LEADERSHIP.

I attended after-school art clubs, continued developing my art and spent five years leading my student council. I was the first student at my school to hold that role for five years.

DRAMA SCHOOL

PERFORMANCE.

I attended drama school for six years and was part of the main cast every single year. That experience helped build my confidence with performance, storytelling and characters.

COLLEGE

TWO DIFFERENT WORLDS.

I attended one college for Games Design and Art and another for Patisserie. Both taught me different ways of approaching creative work.

PATISSERIE

APPARENTLY I ALSO TAUGHT.

While studying patisserie, I ended up helping run the kitchen, organising stock, marking work, planning lessons and teaching other students.

UNIVERSITY

GAMES BECAME THE FOCUS.

I'm now studying Games Design and Production at the University of Salford, bringing together art, storytelling, games and technology.

5 YEARS LEADING STUDENT COUNCIL

I LIKE LEADING THINGS.

Whenever I have been given the opportunity to lead a project, I usually have. I enjoy having a clear vision, organising people around it and making sure the project actually moves forward. That has followed me from school leadership into college and university game projects.

GAMES + LEADERSHIP

During college, I led a team working on an NHS project where we created VR assets intended for use within a game. University projects have continued to develop my production, communication and leadership skills.

HOW I WORK.

CREATIVE INDEPENDENT CURIOUS RELIABLE COLLABORATIVE ADAPTABLE AMBITIOUS PROBLEM SOLVER

INDEPENDENT

I'm comfortable being trusted with a task and figuring out how I want to approach it. I like having responsibility over my work and making sure things actually get finished.

A LEADER

I naturally gravitate towards leadership. I enjoy shaping a project's direction, organising people and helping a team turn an idea into something tangible.

ALWAYS LEARNING

If I don't know how to do something, I would rather learn it than immediately work around it. This is why I keep experimenting with new software and techniques.

CREATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING

I enjoy figuring out how to turn an idea into something real, especially when there isn't an obvious answer. Experimentation is a big part of my process.

01

IDEA

Find the interesting part of the idea and work out what makes it worth developing.

02

EXPLORE

Sketch, experiment, research and try things until something starts to click.

03

BUILD

Turn the idea into actual art, characters, assets, writing or gameplay.

04

REFINE

Keep pushing until the work feels intentional rather than simply finished.

I'm also aware that being creative means constantly having things to improve. I don't expect to know everything, and I'm comfortable recognising where I need to develop further.

05 / 06

OUTSIDE THE WORK.

I'm a massive fan of characters and stories, which is probably unsurprising considering what I ended up studying. I particularly love narrative-heavy games where characters and their relationships are just as important as the gameplay.

Some of my favourite games include Hades, Detroit: Become Human, Cult of the Lamb, the Sherlock Holmes games and the Batman: Arkham series.

I'm also a massive fan of fantasy. I'm not particularly a science-fiction person, although I can absolutely enjoy it when something grabs me.

Outside of games, I'm a lifelong Spider-Man fan and have a particular soft spot for My Little Pony and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. I also enjoy both digital and traditional art.
