HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

THEY / THEM

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, specialising primarily in character art, concept development and visual storytelling.

I've always been drawn to making characters and worlds that feel like they have a life beyond what the player immediately sees. I particularly love creating cute characters with something slightly unsettling hiding underneath, or characters that look harmless until you notice one strange little detail.

Eyes are one of my favourite details to play with. Whether they're exaggerated, hidden, misplaced or quietly staring back at the player from somewhere they shouldn't be, I love using them to make a character feel just a little more interesting.

Art has been a constant part of my life for as long as I can remember, and games have become the place where my interests in art, characters, storytelling and making things all come together.

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is character art, but I enjoy working across different parts of game development and understanding how they connect.

CHARACTER ART

Character concepts, visual development, silhouettes, colour exploration and taking an idea from an early sketch into a finished design. I especially enjoy creating cute, expressive or subtly horrifying characters.

3D ART

3D modelling and asset creation, primarily using Maya, while currently expanding my skills in Blender and ZBrush. I'm particularly interested in pushing towards more detailed, higher-poly character work.

NARRATIVE

Character writing, dialogue, game scripts and narrative-focused design. I particularly enjoy writing characters with distinct personalities, relationships and stories.

GAME DESIGN

Developing gameplay concepts, mechanics and player experiences, particularly where gameplay and narrative can support one another.

ANIMATION

Experience with 2D and 3D animation fundamentals, with a particular interest in developing more advanced character animation and eventually exploring motion capture.

AUDIO & VOICE

Music and sound experimentation, alongside an interest in exploring voice acting and performance for games.

Programming isn't my favourite part of development, but I understand the fundamentals and enjoy knowing enough about the technical side of games to communicate with programmers and understand how my work will actually be used. I also coded this website myself, so apparently I can tolerate programming when there's a pretty website at the end of it.

03 / 06

MY CREATIVE BACKGROUND.

PRIMARY SCHOOL

WHERE IT STARTED.

I was always making something. I attended after-school art clubs and spent a lot of my free time drawing, experimenting and generally finding excuses to be creative.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

ART, DRAMA & LEADERSHIP.

I continued attending art clubs and had already been creating digital art since I was a preteen. I also spent six years attending drama school, where I was part of the main cast every single year.

GCSE & COLLEGE

BUILDING A CREATIVE FOUNDATION.

I studied Art at GCSE before attending one college for Games Design and Art, where I began bringing my visual skills into game development. I later attended a second college to study Patisserie, developing an entirely different creative discipline alongside my work.

UNIVERSITY

GAMES BECAME THE FOCUS.

I'm now studying Games Design and Production at the University of Salford, bringing together my interests in art, storytelling, characters, games and technology.

FIVE YEARS OF STUDENT LEADERSHIP

I led my Student Council throughout all five years of secondary school, becoming the first student in my secondary school to hold that position for the full five-year period. Whenever I was given the opportunity to lead a project throughout my education, I generally took it. Leadership has become something I naturally gravitate towards, particularly when I care about the project and want to help shape where it goes.

PATISSERIE · TEACHING & RESPONSIBILITY

During my patisserie college course, I took on responsibilities that went well beyond being a student. I worked almost like a teaching assistant, helping with stock, organising the entire kitchen, marking work, planning lessons and even teaching students myself. It gave me experience in organisation, communication, responsibility and supporting other people while still working within a practical creative environment.

DRAMA & PERFORMANCE

My six years at drama school also shaped the way I approach characters. Being part of the main cast every year gave me experience with performance, characterisation, collaboration and understanding how personality can be communicated through movement and voice.

HOW I WORK.

CREATIVE INDEPENDENT CURIOUS RELIABLE COLLABORATIVE ADAPTABLE AMBITIOUS PROBLEM SOLVER LEADERSHIP COMMUNICATIVE

INDEPENDENT

I'm comfortable being trusted with a task and working independently to figure out how I want to approach it. I like having responsibility over my work and making sure things actually get finished.

A TEAM PLAYER

I enjoy leading projects and helping shape a team's direction, but I'm equally comfortable working alongside other people and contributing wherever I'm needed.

A NATURAL LEADER

Leadership has been a recurring part of my education, from leading my Student Council for five years to taking leadership roles across projects and helping organise teams. I enjoy giving a project direction while making sure other people have room to contribute.

ALWAYS LEARNING

If I don't know how to do something, I would rather learn it than immediately work around it. I'm continually experimenting with new software, techniques and creative disciplines.

CREATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING

I enjoy figuring out how to turn an idea into something tangible, especially when there isn't an obvious answer. Experimentation is a big part of how I develop my work.

PEOPLE & COMMUNICATION

My experience in drama, student leadership and teaching has made communication a big part of how I work. I enjoy explaining ideas, helping people understand what needs to happen and adapting how I communicate depending on the situation.

I'm also aware that being a creative means constantly having things to improve. I don't expect to know everything, and I'm comfortable recognising where I need to develop further.

05 / 06

OUTSIDE THE WORK.

I'm a massive fan of characters and stories, which is probably unsurprising considering what I ended up studying. I particularly love narrative-heavy games where the characters and their relationships are just as important as the gameplay itself.

Some of my favourite games include Hades, Detroit: Become Human, Cult of the Lamb, the Sherlock Holmes games and the Batman: Arkham series.

I'm especially drawn to fantasy. I'm not particularly a sci-fi person, although there are definitely exceptions. Give me magic, strange creatures, old legends, dramatic characters and an unnecessarily complicated fantasy world and I'm probably interested.

I'm also a massive fan of Merlin, which is probably one of the more obvious signs that I have a weakness for fantasy, magic, complicated relationships and characters making terrible decisions.

Outside of games, I'm a lifelong Spider-Man fan and have a particular soft spot for My Little Pony and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. I enjoy both digital and traditional art and tend to jump between different creative interests depending on what I'm currently obsessed with.
