HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Games Design and Production student at the University of Salford. My main specialism is character art, particularly concepting characters and developing the visual identity that makes them feel like they belong somewhere.

I like making characters that can sit somewhere between charming and subtly unsettling. I love strong silhouettes, strange little details, expressive faces and the feeling that there is more to a character than what you can see immediately.

I've been making art since I was a child, and games became the place where all of those interests could exist together. Art, writing, characters, performance, design and storytelling all end up feeding into the work I make.

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is character art, but I enjoy working across different parts of game development and understanding how they connect.

CHARACTER ART

Character concepts, visual development, silhouettes, colour exploration and taking an idea from an early sketch into a finished design.

3D ART

3D modelling and asset creation, primarily using Maya, while currently expanding my skills in Blender and ZBrush.

NARRATIVE

Character writing, dialogue, game scripts and narrative-focused design. I particularly enjoy writing characters with distinct personalities and relationships.

GAME DESIGN

Developing gameplay concepts, mechanics and player experiences, particularly where gameplay and narrative can support one another.

ANIMATION

Experience with 2D and 3D animation fundamentals, with a particular interest in developing stronger character animation.

AUDIO & VOICE

Music and sound experimentation, alongside an interest in exploring voice acting and performance for games.

Programming isn't my favourite part of development, but I understand the fundamentals and like knowing enough about the technical side to understand how my work will actually be used. I also coded this website myself, which probably says enough about that.

03 / 06

MY CREATIVE BACKGROUND.

PRIMARY SCHOOL

WHERE IT STARTED.

Art clubs, constant drawing and experimenting with anything I could get my hands on. Making things has always been something that came naturally to me.

DRAMA SCHOOL

SIX YEARS OF PERFORMANCE.

During primary and secondary school I attended drama school for six years and was part of the main cast every single year. Performance became another way for me to understand characters and storytelling.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

LEARNING TO LEAD.

I led my student council for five years and was the first student at my school to do so. Whenever I had the opportunity to lead a project, I usually took it.

COLLEGE

TWO VERY DIFFERENT WORLDS.

I attended one college for Games Design and Art and another for Patisserie. The second one taught me a surprising amount about organisation, responsibility and working under pressure.

PATISSERIE

TEACHING, ORGANISING, LEADING.

While studying patisserie I effectively worked as a teaching assistant. I handled stock, organised the kitchen, marked work, planned lessons and taught other students.

UNIVERSITY

GAMES BECAME THE FOCUS.

I'm now studying Games Design and Production at the University of Salford, bringing together my interests in art, storytelling, games and technology.

LEADERSHIP HAS ALWAYS BEEN PART OF HOW I WORK

I've led projects throughout my education, from student council and drama to college and university game projects. I enjoy having responsibility for a project's direction while still being able to work closely with the people around me.

04 / 06

HOW I WORK.

CREATIVE INDEPENDENT CURIOUS RELIABLE COLLABORATIVE ADAPTABLE AMBITIOUS PROBLEM SOLVER

I LIKE LEADING

I'm comfortable taking responsibility for a project, organising work and helping decide where something should go. I naturally gravitate towards leadership because I like turning a vague idea into something everyone can actually work towards.

I GET THINGS DONE

I'm comfortable being trusted with a task and working independently to figure out how I want to approach it. I like having responsibility over my work and making sure things actually get finished.

I LIKE FIGURING THINGS OUT

If I don't know how to do something, I'd rather learn it than immediately work around it. Experimentation and trying something strange are both important parts of my creative process.

I WORK WELL WITH PEOPLE

I enjoy leading projects, but leadership doesn't mean working alone. I like hearing other people's ideas, helping shape them and contributing wherever I'm needed.

I'm also aware that being creative means constantly having things to improve. I don't expect to know everything. I like learning, experimenting and seeing how far I can push an idea.

05 / 06

OUTSIDE THE WORK.

I'm a massive fan of characters and stories, which is probably unsurprising considering what I ended up studying. I particularly love narrative-heavy games where the characters and their relationships are just as important as the gameplay.

Some of my favourite games include Hades, Detroit: Become Human, Cult of the Lamb, the Sherlock Holmes games and the Batman: Arkham series.

I'm also a massive fan of Merlin, Spider-Man, My Little Pony and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

I love both digital and traditional art and tend to jump between different creative interests depending on what I'm currently obsessed with.
