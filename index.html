HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford. My main focus is character art, concept development and visual storytelling, although I have a habit of getting interested in basically every other part of game development too.

I have been making art since I was a kid, from school art clubs and traditional drawing to digital art and eventually game development. I love creating characters that can be genuinely cute one minute and slightly horrifying the next. Fantasy is a huge influence on me, especially when it feels old, strange, theatrical or like it belongs in a storybook.

ALWAYS MAKING

I have a tendency to turn every interest into another creative project. Drawing, modelling, writing, animation, music, baking, gardening. If I can make something out of it, I probably will.

BUILT TO LEAD

I have led projects throughout my education, including leading my student council for five years. I enjoy having responsibility over a project while still working closely with the people around me.

STORIES FIRST

Narrative-heavy games are a particular favourite of mine. I love characters who feel like actual people and worlds that leave you wondering what happened before you arrived.
