01 / About me

Who is Fish?

Hi, I'm Fish.

I'm a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, with a particular interest in creating characters for games.

My main goal is to become a 3D character modeller. I enjoy taking a character from an initial idea and turning it into something that feels like it could actually exist in a game world.

I'm also a 2D artist, and drawing has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I enjoy character illustration, visual development and experimenting with different styles.

Outside of visual art, I have a background in theatre and an interest in voice acting. I haven't put together a voice reel yet, but it's something I'd like to explore further.

I tend to be drawn towards characters and worlds that are a little bit strange, often mixing something cute with something unsettling. Fairytales, fantasy, unusual creatures and character-driven stories are a big inspiration for me.

My direction

What I'm working towards

I want to build a career creating characters for games, developing my technical modelling skills while keeping my illustration and storytelling interests involved.

Longer term, I'd love to explore creative direction and have more involvement in shaping the visual identity of projects.

Based in: United Kingdom

Currently studying: Game Design and Production

University: University of Salford

06 / My background

How I got here

Art has always been there.

I've been drawing and making things for as long as I can remember. Studying Art at GCSE helped me develop those interests and think more seriously about creative work.

Finding my way into games.

I continued exploring creative subjects before moving into Games Design and Production at the University of Salford. Games bring together a lot of the things I enjoy: characters, art, stories and technology.

Working with other people.

Through college and university projects, I've had experience working in teams and taking on leadership responsibilities. One college project involved an NHS-related VR project where our team created assets intended for use within a game.

Outside of modelling

I have also spent several years involved in theatre, which is part of why voice acting interests me.

I enjoy narrative-heavy games, fantasy, fairytales, character-driven stories, drawing, digital painting and generally making things.

I like creative work that has a bit of personality to it. If something is a little strange, whimsical or unsettling, I'm probably interested.
