HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

PRONOUNS THEY / THEM

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, with my main focus being character art, concept development and visual storytelling.

I've been making art for as long as I can remember. I started with after-school art clubs and traditional drawing, moved into digital art as a preteen, and have continued developing my skills through GCSE, college and university.

I'm particularly interested in characters that are cute, strange or quietly unsettling. I love taking something that initially looks friendly and adding small details that make you look twice.

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is character art, but I enjoy working across different parts of game development and understanding how they connect.

CHARACTER ART

Character concepts, visual development, silhouettes, colour exploration and taking an idea from an early sketch into a finished design. I especially enjoy creating cute characters and characters with subtle horror elements.

3D ART

3D modelling and asset creation, primarily using Maya, while continuing to expand my skills in Blender and ZBrush. I'm particularly interested in developing stronger high-poly character work.

NARRATIVE

Character writing, dialogue, game scripts and narrative-focused design. I enjoy creating characters with distinct personalities, relationships and stories that support the wider game.

GAME DESIGN

Developing gameplay concepts, mechanics and player experiences, particularly where gameplay and narrative can support one another.

ANIMATION

Experience with 2D and 3D animation fundamentals, with an interest in pushing character animation further and eventually exploring motion capture.

AUDIO & VOICE

Music and sound experimentation alongside an interest in voice acting, performance and how audio can help establish character and atmosphere.

Programming isn't my favourite part of development, but I understand the fundamentals and enjoy knowing enough about the technical side of games to understand how my work will actually be used. I also coded this website myself, so I can survive it when necessary.

03 / 06

MY CREATIVE BACKGROUND.

PRIMARY SCHOOL

WHERE IT STARTED.

I attended after-school art clubs and was constantly drawing, making things and experimenting creatively. Art became something I naturally kept returning to.

DRAMA SCHOOL

SIX YEARS OF PERFORMANCE.

During primary and secondary school, I attended drama school for six years and was part of the main cast every single year. This gave me experience with performance, characters, dialogue and working as part of a production.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

LEARNING TO LEAD.

I led my student council for five years and became the first student at my secondary school to hold the role for that length of time. Whenever I was given the opportunity to lead a project, I took it.

COLLEGE

GAMES, ART & PATISSERIE.

I attended two colleges. One focused on Games Design and Art, where I developed my creative and game development skills, while the other focused on patisserie and gave me a completely different practical discipline.

PATISSERIE

MORE THAN BAKING.

During my patisserie course, I worked in a teaching assistant-style role. I handled stock, organised the kitchen, marked work, helped plan lessons and taught students, giving me experience with responsibility, organisation and supporting other people.

UNIVERSITY

GAMES BECAME THE FOCUS.

I'm now studying Games Design and Production at the University of Salford, bringing together my interests in art, storytelling, games, design and production.

PROJECT LEADERSHIP

Leadership has been a consistent part of my education. From student council and drama productions to college projects and university development teams, I've regularly found myself taking responsibility for organising people, shaping ideas and making sure work moves forward.

04 / 06

HOW I WORK.

CREATIVE INDEPENDENT CURIOUS RELIABLE COLLABORATIVE ADAPTABLE AMBITIOUS PROBLEM SOLVER

INDEPENDENT

I'm comfortable being trusted with a task and working independently to figure out how I want to approach it. I like having responsibility over my work and making sure things actually get finished.

A TEAM PLAYER

I enjoy leading projects and helping shape a team's direction, but I'm equally comfortable working alongside other people and contributing wherever I'm needed.

ALWAYS LEARNING

If I don't know how to do something, I would rather learn it than immediately work around it. I'm continually experimenting with new software, techniques and creative disciplines.

CREATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING

I enjoy figuring out how to turn an idea into something tangible, especially when there isn't an obvious answer. Experimentation is a big part of how I develop my work.

I'm also aware that being a creative means constantly having things to improve. I don't expect to know everything, and I'm comfortable recognising where I need to develop further.

05 / 06

OUTSIDE THE WORK.

I'm a massive fan of characters, stories and fantasy. I particularly love narrative-heavy games where the characters and their relationships are just as important as the gameplay itself.

Some of my favourite games include Hades, Detroit: Become Human, Cult of the Lamb, the Sherlock Holmes games and the Batman: Arkham series.

I'm also a massive fan of Merlin, alongside Spider-Man, My Little Pony and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Fantasy is a particular favourite of mine. I'm not usually drawn to science fiction, although there are always exceptions.

Outside of games and art, I enjoy baking and gardening. I also have a bearded dragon called Lord Bartholomew, who I've had since around 2021.
