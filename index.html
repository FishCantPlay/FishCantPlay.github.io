HI, I'M FISH.

GAME DESIGNER · CHARACTER ARTIST · STORYTELLER

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, specialising primarily in character art, concept development and visual storytelling.

I've always been drawn to creating characters and worlds that feel like they have a life beyond what the player immediately sees. I particularly enjoy designing characters that can sit somewhere between charming and subtly unsettling.

Art has been a constant part of my life for as long as I can remember, and games have become the place where those interests come together.

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is character art, but I enjoy working across different parts of game development and understanding how they connect.

CHARACTER ART

Character concepts, visual development, silhouettes, colour exploration and taking an idea from an early sketch into a finished design.

3D ART

3D modelling and asset creation, primarily using Maya, while currently expanding my skills in Blender and ZBrush.

NARRATIVE

Character writing, dialogue, game scripts and narrative-focused design. I particularly enjoy writing characters with distinct personalities and relationships.

GAME DESIGN

Developing gameplay concepts, mechanics and player experiences, particularly where gameplay and narrative can support one another.

ANIMATION

Experience with 2D and 3D animation fundamentals, with a particular interest in developing more advanced character animation.

AUDIO & VOICE

Music and sound experimentation, alongside an interest in exploring voice acting and performance for games.

Programming isn't my favourite part of development, but I understand the fundamentals and enjoy knowing enough about the technical side of games to communicate with programmers and understand how my work will actually be used. I also coded this website myself, so apparently I can tolerate programming when there's a pretty website at the end of it.

03 / 06

MY CREATIVE BACKGROUND.

PRIMARY SCHOOL

WHERE IT STARTED.

Art clubs, constant drawing and experimenting with anything I could get my hands on. Making things has always been something that came naturally to me.

SECONDARY SCHOOL

ART, DRAMA & LEADERSHIP.

I attended after-school art clubs, studied Art at GCSE and spent six years at drama school, where I was part of the main cast every year. I also led my student council for five years, becoming the first student at my school to do so.

COLLEGE

TWO VERY DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS.

I attended two colleges, studying Games Design and Art at one and Patisserie at the other. Patisserie gave me another creative discipline, while games allowed me to bring my art and storytelling interests together.

UNIVERSITY

GAMES BECAME THE FOCUS.

I'm now studying Games Design and Production at the University of Salford, developing my skills across character art, 3D, narrative, design and production.

PROJECT LEADERSHIP

Whenever I have been given the opportunity to lead a project throughout my education, I've tended to take it. During college, I led a team working on an NHS project where we created VR assets intended for use within a game. At one point during my patisserie studies, I also effectively worked as a teaching assistant, organising stock and the kitchen, marking work, planning lessons and helping teach.

04 / 06

HOW I WORK.

CREATIVE INDEPENDENT CURIOUS RELIABLE COLLABORATIVE ADAPTABLE AMBITIOUS PROBLEM SOLVER

INDEPENDENT

I'm comfortable being trusted with a task and working independently to figure out how I want to approach it. I like having responsibility over my work and making sure things actually get finished.

A TEAM PLAYER

I enjoy leading projects and helping shape a team's direction, but I'm equally comfortable working alongside other people and contributing wherever I'm needed.

ALWAYS LEARNING

If I don't know how to do something, I would rather learn it than immediately work around it. This is why I'm continually experimenting with new software, techniques and creative disciplines.

CREATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING

I enjoy figuring out how to turn an idea into something tangible, especially when there isn't an obvious answer. Experimentation is a big part of how I develop my work.

I'm also aware that being a creative means constantly having things to improve. I don't expect to know everything, and I'm comfortable recognising where I need to develop further.

05 / 06

OUTSIDE THE WORK.

I'm a massive fan of characters and stories, which is probably unsurprising considering what I ended up studying. I particularly love narrative-heavy games where the characters and their relationships are just as important as the gameplay itself.

Some of my favourite games include Hades, Detroit: Become Human, Cult of the Lamb, the Sherlock Holmes games and the Batman: Arkham series.

Outside of games, I'm a lifelong Spider-Man fan and have a particular soft spot for My Little Pony and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. I'm also a massive fan of Merlin.

I enjoy both digital and traditional art and tend to jump between different creative interests depending on what I'm currently obsessed with.

Continuing to grow as a versatile game developer while developing a stronger specialism in character art and narrative.

Ultimately, I want to make things that people remember. Whether that's a character, a story, a strange little game mechanic or an entire world, I'm most interested in the parts of game development that make something feel like it has a personality of its own.
