HI, I'M FISH.

I'm Fish, a Game Designer and Artist who likes making characters, strange little worlds and games that sit somewhere between cute, unsettling and a little bit weird.

I enjoy working across character art, game design, 2D animation and 3D, with a particular interest in creating characters and worlds with their own personality.

I'm always interested in projects that let me experiment with storytelling, visual design and the stranger side of games.
