01 / 06

HI, I'M FISH.

3D CHARACTER MODELLER · 2D ARTIST · STORYTELLER

THEY / THEM ARTIST GAME DESIGN STUDENT

I'm Fish Heard, a nonbinary Game Design and Production student at the University of Salford, specialising primarily in character art, concept development and visual storytelling.

I've always been drawn to creating characters and worlds that feel like they have a life beyond what the player immediately sees. Whether that's through visual design, dialogue, narrative or the little details that make a world feel believable, I enjoy finding the things that give a project its personality.

Art has been a constant part of my life for as long as I can remember, and games have become the place where all of those interests come together.

02 / 06

WHAT I DO.

My main focus is 3D character modelling, but I enjoy working across different creative disciplines. I like understanding how all the different parts of a game come together.

3D CHARACTER ART

This is the direction I want to pursue professionally. I currently use Maya and have recently started learning Blender and ZBrush. I'm still at the beginning of developing my 3D skills, but I'm particularly interested in bringing characters from concept into 3D.

2D ART & ILLUSTRATION

Drawing, character concepts, anatomy, visual development and experimenting with different styles. I love being able to communicate a character through their appearance and design.

VOICE ACTING

Another creative direction I'd like to explore is voice acting, particularly character performance. I haven't published any voice acting work yet, but it's something I want to develop alongside my visual work.

STORYTELLING

Character personalities, relationships, worldbuilding and the small details that make fictional worlds feel interesting and believable.

MY BEARDED DRAGON

03 / 06

MY LITTLE COMPANION.

Not everything I do revolves around university or making art. I have my own little life outside of all that too!

One very important part of that is my bearded dragon. He's definitely worth having his own little place on my website.

I enjoy spending time with my pets and looking after them. I also find animals, their anatomy and their personalities really interesting, which is something that often feeds into my creative interests.

I think it's important to show the person behind the portfolio as well as the work.

04 / 06

WHEN I'M NOT DRAWING...

I have plenty of interests outside of making artwork. I enjoy games, stories, character-driven media and anything that gives me something interesting to think about or create.

I also have an interest in theatre and performance, which is part of why voice acting is something I want to explore further.

I like collecting inspiration from different places, whether that's a character design, a film, a game, an interesting creature or just something I happen to find interesting.

A lot of my hobbies end up influencing my artwork in one way or another.

ALWAYS LEARNING.

I'm currently studying Game Design and Production at the University of Salford. My time at university has given me the opportunity to experiment with different areas of game development and work on projects with other people.
